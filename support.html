FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Orders usually arrive within 3 to 5 working days.

Is delivery free?

Yes, on orders over Rs. 10000. Below that there is a flat Rs. 500 fee.

Can I return a toy?

Unopened toys can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

How do I track my collection?

Use the Wishlist page. You can mark each toy as Interested, Owned or Not interested.
